# Community conduct

Participate with respect and good faith. Focus criticism on technical choices and their effects, assume mistakes can be corrected, and do not harass, discriminate against, threaten, or disclose another person's private information. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through GitHub where possible. Public issues are for product and engineering discussion, not private conduct reports.
